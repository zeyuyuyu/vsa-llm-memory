  - T2-1 Bouthillier et al. 2021 (arXiv:2103.03098): hyperparameter selection is a major variance source and should be
    randomised. SUPPORTS the design (configuration re-selected on dev per map) and the observation that selection, not
    the map, moved the published number most (B36 map +6.77 vs new maps +8.1 to +8.6).
  - T2-4 Saravanan, Berman, Sober 2020 (hierarchical bootstrap, NBDT 3(5)): resampling level by level for nested data.
    SUPPORTS the two-level (maps x entity clusters) interval as the method.
  - T2-9 Sutherland & Schneider 2015 (arXiv:1506.02785): error of random Fourier features across draws concentrates
    with dimension. CONTEXT / SUPPORTS the small between-map SD (0.23 points) at d of 10^3-10^4.
  - T2-2 Dodge et al. 2020, T2-3 Reimers & Gurevych 2017: seed variance can change conclusions. CONTEXT (here it does not).
  - No precedent found for a seed-and-selection-randomised equal-bits comparison of a VSA store with classical memories.
