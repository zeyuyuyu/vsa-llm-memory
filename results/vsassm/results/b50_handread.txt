- Addition is not unfamiliar to DISC's reader: every addition item is read as BITWISE OR (250 of 250 in the forced-open
  run, seed 0; the VSA reader also names OR on all 250, but with similarity 0.61-0.79 at the 5th-95th percentiles, below its 0.8 gate). DISC's
  scores for addition sit inside the trained-type range: MSP log-odds median 9.2 (trained-type medians 11.3-13.0, dev
  threshold 7.17), Mahalanobis d^2 median 10,020 (trained-type medians 3,570-6,820; threshold 19,727). So the standard gates
  (matched to the VSA gate's 99.6% dev open rate) open on 94.5% (MSP) to 99.6% (Mahalanobis) of addition items.
- What happens when they open (MSP, seed 0; 240 open items): DISC read both operands exactly on 226; the LLM then
  stated DISC's OR result on 178 and wrote "x + y =" without finishing in 6 tokens on 22; 16 were scored correct, 11
  of them because x OR y happens to equal x + y (no shared bits). Same picture for Mahalanobis (249 open; 185 stated
  the OR result; 18 correct, 13 by OR = sum). The failure is the module's confident wrong program, not a read error.
- Integer division: every standard gate closes on 250 of 250 items (DISC reads them mostly as multiplication with low
  scores: MSP log-odds median 1.0, d^2 median 49,000); the arm then equals BASE (240 of 250 correct in both).
- Trained-type items the gates close on (MSP 9, Mahalanobis 3, seed 0): all 9 / 3 had a CORRECT module answer, and
  BASE was right on only 2 / 1 of them, so each close on a trained item costs accuracy (TR 0.9275 for MSP vs 0.9298).
  Typical closed items are edge cases (y = 1 in multiplication, x = y in OR).
- VSA gate on addition (seed 0): 9 of 250 open, all read as OR with similarity 0.800-0.882, all 9 wrong. So the VSA
  gate's rejection is a margin in a continuous score, not an exact property: addition similarities sit just below
  the 0.8 cut (q95 0.789).
- Decision check on the printed sample: for every printed held-out item the logged gate equals score >= tau, and the
  stated output matches the module answer or BASE as the gate implies (0 composition mismatches on 7,500 items per
  gate in the verdict).
