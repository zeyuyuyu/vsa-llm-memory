## Robustness notes
- Accuracies of every arm reproduce the paper / verdict files at three decimals, and the question-level CIs reproduce
  the paper's printed intervals exactly (same procedure, same seed).
- Seeds 0-4 for the cluster bootstrap: K = 4 upper bound -0.039 to -0.042; K = 8 upper bound -0.011 to -0.012
  (lower -0.321 to -0.333). Stable, but at K = 8 only 12 sites (3 per scenario) support it, and percentile cluster
  intervals with this few clusters tend to be too narrow; the 8-day exclusion of zero is marginal.
- Per-site sign (sum over a site's questions of ours-correct minus GPT-correct): K = 4, GPT ahead at 10 sites, tied 16,
  ours ahead 2 (28 sites); K = 8, GPT ahead 6, tied 5, ours ahead 1 (12 sites); GPT's lead comes mostly from the
  warehouse sites (K = 4: 5 of the 10 sites; K = 8: 3 of the 6 sites).
- One-day comparisons: the cluster intervals are 18 % (impact) and 57 % (window) wider than the question-level ones (lower bounds move
  from -0.061 to -0.073 for impact and from -0.067 to -0.090 for window, against GPT-6.1 Sol), so "GPT-6.1 Sol up to
  seven points ahead" becomes "up to seven (impact) / nine (window) points ahead".
