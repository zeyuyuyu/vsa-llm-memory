# B21 verdict (prereg/B21_ruler_vsa_prereg.md + amendments 1-2): RULER with a sharded Fourier HRR store, 2026-11-14

Data: the B19 test sets (official RULER scripts, Qwen3 tokenizer, seed 42, 100 samples per task and length).
System: B19's reader (prompted Qwen3-8B, no RULER training) and, in place of B19's fact table, a sharded Fourier HRR
store (d = 8192, at most 16 pairs per vector on average, separate key and value codebooks, clean-up threshold 0.5).
Both stores answered from the same reader output in the same run. CWE / FWE / QA do not use the memory; their B19
predictions are reused for the 13-task averages.
Compute (amendment 2): PACE, paid account; jobs 15493950 (4k-32k, H200) and 15493872 (64k/128k, mostly L40S, one
A100-80GB). 5,400 samples, all complete, no errors. 24.3 GPU-hours in all (sacct elapsed, summed over the 22
array tasks); 12.9 GPU-hours for the 900 samples at 128k (51.6 GPU-s per sample).

Literature check: HRR capacity theory (Plate 1995, 2003; Frady et al. 2018) predicts exact read-out when the load per
vector is well below d / (2 ln N); 38 pairs at d = 8192 is far inside it (supporting). A quick search found no
published RULER-128k result above 94.9 outside the leaderboard (weak evidence; not exhaustive).
