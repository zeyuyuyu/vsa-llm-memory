## Notes (by me, after reading the 40 queries above)

- Both selected cells are V = 16 at 16 bits per fact, where VSAP falls far behind the random-feature store (qwen8
  -14.1, e5 -21.3 points). The "only VSAS right" lists are full of near-copies of the label that VSAP misreads: 'Day the
  Fish Came Out', 'Endre Balint', 'Johann Heinrich Ross', 'Barrie George Aitchison', 'State of Bahia' (qwen8); 'A
  Brokedown Melody', 'Egypt Eyalet', 'Jack Mackenzie', 'Western Hockey League' (e5). These are the queries any working
  store should answer, so the failure is in the read, not in hard aliases. That fits the mechanism stated in the
  pre-registration (Q2): with Hadamard value codes the score of a value is a signed sum over 16 residue classes of
  coordinates, and on principal coordinates each class sees a fixed 1/16 of the principal directions (every 16th),
  so each of the 16 sign decisions is made from a small, fixed slice of the key; random features give every class
  d / 16 projections of the whole key.
- The "only VSAP right" lists also contain near-copies ('Stefan the Firstcrowned', 'Bruce James Crowder', 'Sheffield
  United Football Club', 'Vancouver Caps FC'), so VSAP is not broken, only weaker at V = 16.
- No sign of a scoring artifact: gold values, predictions and labels are consistent.
