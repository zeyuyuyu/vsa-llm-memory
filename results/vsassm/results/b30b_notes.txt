## (b) BABILong notepad (the same reader's events as text)
Registered readings: NB1 and NB2 "memory adds"; the conservative rule (every arm of a size) "memory adds" for 8B and
for 1.7B, at 128k, 512k and 1M. G-B passed at all three lengths (re-run memory = banked B18 on 500/500, 125/125,
125/125), and a plain-dict tracker reproduces every memory answer.
Numbers (QA1-5; memory 99.8 at 128k, 100.0 at 512k and 1M; difference in points, exact McNemar p; in every arm the
discordant pairs all favour the memory except one sample):
- 128k (n = 500): Qwen3-8B thinking 78.6 (+21.2, p = 2.5e-32; qa3 43, qa5 69), Qwen3-1.7B thinking 59.8 (+40.0,
  p = 1.2e-60), Qwen3-8B without thinking 58.6, Qwen3-1.7B without thinking 34.2; the reader itself as answerer 45.4 /
  25.2 (it answers with event lists: a format failure).
- 512k (n = 125): 8B thinking 83.2 (+16.8, p = 9.5e-7), 1.7B thinking 56.8 (+43.2).
- 1M (n = 125): 8B thinking 77.6 (+22.4, p = 7.5e-9), 1.7B thinking 57.6 (+42.4).
- Notepads are short (median 13 lines at 128k, 27 at 1M); the errors are reasoning over a changing state (qa2 carried
  objects, qa3 "before", qa4 inverse relations) and the recency convention on qa5 (listing every receiver). Counting
  "several names including the right one" as right (post hoc) lifts 8B at 128k only to 85.4.
Suggested sentence (Results, beside the B23 baseline): "Given exactly the events our reader extracted, written as a
notepad of short sentences, Qwen3-8B answers 78.6% of the QA1-5 questions at 128k (83.2% at 512k, 77.6% at 1M) and
Qwen3-1.7B 59.8%, against the memory's 99.8-100% on the same events (P < 1e-6 at every length): the extraction alone
does not carry the result; the rules over the exact store do."

## Caveats to state
- One extra seed only; location's seed spread (0.06) is as large as the decoding effect, so the S32 numbers carry
  sampling noise of that size (the two-seed mean is the steadier figure).
- The notepad arms are open 1.7B / 8B models; frontier models reading the raw text reach 96.8 / 97.6 at 128k on the
  first 25 per task (B18 references), so the notepad result is about small and mid-size models.
- Part (a) on vLLM 0.11 with NCCL all-reduce (custom all-reduce and NCCL P2P fail on these PCIe A800s); the engine
  control shows this does not move the greedy numbers.
