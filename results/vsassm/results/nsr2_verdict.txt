# 2026-11-09. Held-out nsr2: 40 fresh logs (seeds 500+, generated after the prereg; fallbacks warehouse 2.7%, biobank 3.9%,

## Conclusion
The useful part of the engine is the neural one: a reliability model over observable features of the log (which channel
wrote a fact, how many moves a message carried) separates wrong facts from right ones (AUROC 0.78 on fresh logs) and, on
held-out data, does point at the wrong fact (permuting the scores costs 0.07 AUROC). Its plainest use, the lowest
reliability among the facts an answer depends on, flags wrong answers as well as anything tried (AUROC 0.70). The
symbolic layer on top, exact probabilistic inference over type-consistent alternative places, gave +0.09 and +0.03 over
that baseline in development and -0.02 on held-out data (95% interval -0.085 to +0.049): no measurable added value for
knowing when an answer is wrong. It also never changes an answer (MAP = deterministic). Storing the soft state in a VSA
superposition loses 0.10 AUROC to crosstalk at D = 2048.
Error that no confidence signal here catches: an item misread into another place, which leaves the asked place's answer
empty or short with nothing pointing at it (home garden/garage, ops db-11 in nsr2): the engine would have to suspect a
fact elsewhere.
Power: 19 wrong answers out of 230, so AUROC differences below ~0.08 are not resolvable on one split.

## Literature check (conclusion -> literature)
SUPPORTED in direction: Hendrycks & Gimpel (ICLR 2017, abstract opened) find the maximum softmax probability an effective
baseline for detecting misclassified examples that other methods "can sometimes" surpass; here the analogous direct
signal (minimum fact reliability) is not surpassed by probabilistic propagation. The review's neuro-symbolic engines
(Scallop, DeepProbLog; docs/neurosymbolic_review.md Part III.6) report answer accuracy, not selective prediction over a
read state; NO PRECEDENT FOUND for this exact comparison.

## What next
The engine's open problem is the missed-item error (a fact elsewhere should be suspected). Candidate: re-reading messages
that mention the asked place, or per-place reliability, as an NSR2b prereg. The learned fact reliability is worth keeping
as a product feature ("ask a human to confirm these facts"), with min-reliability as the answer-level flag.
