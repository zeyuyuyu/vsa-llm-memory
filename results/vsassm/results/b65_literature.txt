[LG25] Levy, A. A. & Geva, M. Language Models Encode Numbers Using Digit Representations in Base 10. NAACL 2025
  (short), pp. 385-395; arXiv:2410.11781 (snippet only: aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-short.33, arxiv.org/html/2410.11781v2).
  Per-digit circular probes on the hidden state of a number recover each base-10 digit (about 0.91 per digit for
  Llama 3 8B, layers 3 and up); errors are "close in digit space" but far in value space.
  -> SUPPORTS B65's OPS result that every module reads the operands almost exactly (read exact 0.992-1.000) from the
     states at the operand tokens, and the digit-space character of the LAST misreads in the sample reading (one digit
     off: 460 -> 450, 748 -> 738, 493 -> 593).
