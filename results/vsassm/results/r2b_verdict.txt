mother Sharon and his brother Oliver" -> Oliver read as Sharon's brother) and missed first sentences. Longer
concatenated training stories fixed the length effect only partly (recall .38 -> .66 at k = 10); the test stories'
sentences are more complex than the training stories', and a 1.7B reader trained on the simple ones does not read the
complex ones. The prompted 8B reader with thinking keeps recall near .9 at every length.
Conclusion: a small fine-tuned reader is not the way to close the CLUTRR gap; the reported CLUTRR result stays R1's
(prompted Qwen3-8B reader, 0.717). The memory is not the limit (gold facts 0.973).
