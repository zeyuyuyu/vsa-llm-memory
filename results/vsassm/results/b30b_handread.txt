## Part (a), location (414): S32 R1 vs the published greedy run
114 questions differ in correctness: S32 right on 67, the published run right on 47 (net +20 = D1 +0.048). Only 9 of
the 114 had hit the 8,192-token limit in the published run (S32 right on 7 of them), so about 5 of the net 20 come from
the larger budget and the rest from sampling instead of greedy decoding reading the same log differently. No S32 run
reached the 32,768 budget (longest reasoning 13,535 tokens). Reading 20 of the 114 (random, seed 0): every miss on
either side is a misread state, never a format problem: the wrong freezer or rack at the end of a box > rack > freezer
chain (biobank-301-1, -305-5, -304-4, -307-7), a pallet put back to an earlier slot or the dock (warehouse-301-1,
-305-5, -306-6), a cluster or primary taken from a stale bot line instead of the engineer's later message (ops-305-5,
ops-301-1, ops-300-0), a household item placed by the camera rather than by what was heard (home-300-0, -301-1,
-309-9). Sampling fixes some greedy misreads and makes others; the net is small against the system's 0.959.

## Part (a), impact (230) and window (460): S32 R1 vs the published greedy run
- The questions the published run lost to the 8,192-token limit stay hard with 32,768 tokens and sampling: of the 48
  impact questions that hit the limit, S32 R1 answers 5 correctly (the published forced answers: 3); of the 73 window
  questions, 2 (published forced: 4). S32 R1 itself reached the 32,768 budget on 1 impact and 3 window questions only:
  the extra budget is used and the reasoning ends, but it ends on a wrong state.
- Discordant questions: impact 35 (S32 right 20, published right 15), window 48 (22 vs 26). Read 10 of each (random,
  seed 0). Every miss on either side is a misread or incomplete state, not a format or parse problem: a pallet that
  moved into or out of the aisle during the day missed (warehouse-500-0, -508-8), a database primary placed on the
  wrong host so services are added or dropped (ops-400-0, -401-1, -503-3, -500-0, -505-5, ops-405-5), a service's
  region taken from a stale deploy line (ops-507-7, -502-2), a home item's room missed or added (home-401-1, -406-6,
  -408-8, -504-4). On the ambiguous home questions ("Grandpa's things") S32 sometimes answers with Grandpa's own items
  only (home-401-1: "cane" for cane, slippers, tablet), the wording defect the NSR1 verdict recorded.
- So the two decodings make different single misreads on the same long, overwritten logs; neither tracks the state
  well. The budget and the decoding were not what kept Qwen3-32B at 0.2-0.3 on these sets.

## Part (b): BABILong 128k, samples where the memory and the notepad disagree (read by hand 2026-11-18)

Every disagreement goes one way: the memory is right on all 106 (q8T) and 200 (q17T) samples where the arms differ.
Automatic classification of every notepad error (b30b_handread.py babclass) plus reading of 20+ cases in full
(notepad, answer and the end of the reasoning):

q8T (107 errors at 128k):
- qa3 "Where was X before Y?" (57 errors): the notepads are long (up to 152 lines; the qa3 stories are long), and the
  model loses the object's trajectory: it attaches the object to the wrong carrier, or forgets that a carried object
  moves with its carrier. 13 of the 45 single-label errors name the place before the FIRST visit to Y rather than the
  most recent one (the bAbI gold and our rule use the most recent; the model's reasoning sometimes says "for the first
  time"); 7 give no place.
- qa5 "Who received X / who gave X to Y" (31 errors): 28 list several names with the right one among them ("Mary,
  Fred"): the record has several gives of the same object, and the model lists them all instead of naming the most
  recent, which the official rule scores wrong. A task-convention error, not a reading error; frontier models given
  the raw text follow the convention (B18 references 96.8 / 97.6 at 128k).
- qa2 "Where is the football?" (10 errors), on SHORT clean notepads (12-33 lines): genuine state-tracking errors, e.g.
  128k|qa2|84 (12 lines): "John took the football. John went to the garden. ... John went to the bathroom." -> the
  model answers garden, forgetting that the football moved with John; 128k|qa2|39: drop order misread (bedroom
  instead of bathroom).
- qa4 (8 errors): inverse relations misread ("What is the office west of?" answered with what is west of the office),
  or two places listed.
- qa1 (1 error): 128k|qa1|92, the model attached two book-text events ("He went to the party", "He went to the town")
  to John and missed "John went to the hallway". The notepad includes the reader's false events from the book text
  (e.g. "Fred thompson went to the new york.", "Brinsmade went to the hospital."); the memory is keyed by name and never
  reads them for a bAbI question, the model has to ignore them.
q17T: the same kinds, more of them (qa2 57, qa3 78, qa4 34 errors), plus answers that list every place a person
  visited ("bedroom, office, bathroom, garden").
Reader with its LoRA (rdT, rdN): mostly a FORMAT failure: on qa2/qa3 it answers with an event list
  ({"events": [["go", ...]]}) or "nothing" (the adapter was trained to write events); these arms say nothing about
  reasoning and do not change the conservative reading (q17T is the best 1.7B arm).
Without thinking (q8N, q17N): many more single wrong labels on qa2-qa4; q17N often echoes the instruction
  ("a place, a name, an object").

Post-hoc sensitivity (not registered): counting "several labels with the target among them" as right raises q8T at
128k from 78.6 to 85.4 (+34), still 14.4 points below the memory.
