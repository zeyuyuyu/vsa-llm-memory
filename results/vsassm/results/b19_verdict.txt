# B19 verdict (prereg/B19_ruler_prereg.md + amendment 1): RULER, 13 tasks, 4k-128k, 2026-10-22

Data: official RULER scripts, Qwen3 tokenizer, seed 42, 100 samples per task and length (the leaderboard uses 500).
Development used a separate seed-7 set only.
Ours:
  - Reader: Qwen3-8B, prompted, no RULER training. Facts are read from ~250-character chunks into a fact table.
  - CWE / FWE: a word counter (no model).
  - QA: query-aware relevance reading, then an answer from the relevant documents.
Reference: the same Qwen3-8B answering directly (RULER base template + answer prefix; YaRN x4 at 64k / 128k).
Compute: 4k-32k ran on the A800 (free GPUs). 64k / 128k ran on PACE H200, paid account, 23.4 GPU-hours for both
systems.
Scoring: official string_match_all / string_match_part. The merged outputs were rescored locally and agree.

Caveats (must accompany any quote):
  1. CWE / FWE are counted by the memory's counter, not read by a model. Leaderboard models do them by reading.
  2. One example in the fact prompt (uuid key / value, "special secret ids for ...") is close to RULER's needle
     wording. It was chosen on dev by recall (amendment 1).
  3. QA is query-aware: the reader sees the question when reading the documents.
  4. n = 100 per task (the leaderboard uses 500). The reader here is Qwen3-8B, not the 1.7B BABILong student.
Reader cost at 128k:
  - About 3,000 chunk reads per sample.
  - Repeated haystack chunks are cached: niah_single_1 at 128k needs 109 distinct reads for 100 samples.
  - Needle-dense tasks are not cached: multikey_2 needs 198,852 reads and 9.6M generated tokens for 100 samples.
