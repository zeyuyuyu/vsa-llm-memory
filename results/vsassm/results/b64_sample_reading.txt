Qwen3-8B, M = 256, V = 2, 8 bits per fact (store first design 60.7, SDMt 71.8; SDMt = whiten / labels / a = 2 /
value-bit counters / ternary / all facts). Discordant: 1,308 queries only SDMt right, 766 only the store right.
- With two values a wrong answer is right half the time by luck, so the discordant sets carry coin flips on both
  sides: several "SDMt only" cases have cos(alias, label) of 0.03-0.17 and share no active location with the label
  ('Dayanidhi' -> 'Krishna', 'Mingyan' -> 'Koxinga', 'American East' -> 'Eastern United States'); neither memory has
  any signal there and SDMt's answer is the default value or the sign of an unrelated location.
- The informative SDMt wins share an active location with the label: 'Mauricio Luciano Lollo' -> 'Luciano Lollo'
  (both reach the training label 'Luciano Vincenzoni'), 'Neighborhood Watch' -> 'The Watch' ('The Night Watch'),
  'John Joseph Myers' -> 'John J. Myers' ('Richard Myers'). The training labels act as a fixed codebook of named
  cells; an alias and its label fall into the same cell when they share a salient token, and a cell holds the votes
  of only the few stored facts near it, so the read is nearly free of cross-talk.
- The store's wins are aliases whose label is reached by graded similarity but not by the same cell: 'Jørgine Anna
  Krog' -> 'Gina Krog' (alias cells 'Jörg A. Eggers', 'Jörg Bergmeister'), 'Jean Henri Roos' -> 'Johann Heinrich Roos',
  'Makaziwe Mandela-Amuah' -> 'Makaziwe Mandela' (share 1 of 2, outvoted). Quantization to a cell loses these.
- Nothing in the 20 cases looks like a leak: the activated locations are other entities' training names, never the
  stored label or a string of it (the disjointness check: no location has cosine >= 0.9999 to any dev / test code).

e5-base-v2, M = 256, V = 16, 8 bits per fact (store 23.9, SDMt 29.9; SDMt = center / labels / a = 1 / one presence bit
per value / all facts). Discordant 969 vs 685.
- Every sampled SDMt win has the alias and the label in the same single cell (1 of 1) except one ('Vir' -> 'Mahavira',
  a lucky vote): 'Mark Vande Hei' / 'Mark T. Vande Hei' -> cell 'Mark Allen'; 'Zablati' / 'Záblatí' (cosine 1.00 under
  e5's accent stripping) -> 'Zakarpattia Oblast'. With 16 values the store's read of these near-duplicates still
  fails through cross-talk (cosine 0.83-1.00 and a wrong answer), whereas a cell holding one or two facts answers
  exactly: at V = 16 the cell structure, not the similarity, carries the win.
- Every sampled store win has the alias and the label in different cells: 'Geneva' -> 'Canton of Geneva' (alias cell
  'France'), 'Republic of Albania' -> 'Albania' ('North Macedonia'), 'Mawlana Rumi' -> 'Rumi'. These are boundary
  failures of a hard nearest-cell assignment, the same failure as an LSH or PQ bucket, at a much finer, data-placed
  partition.
- Reading: SDMt at the chosen settings (a = 1-2) is a count sketch whose buckets are the Voronoi cells of the
  training labels; its advantage over B36's LSH / PQ sketches is that the cells follow the codes' distribution
  (whitened), so an alias usually lands in its label's cell; its losses are the aliases that cross a cell boundary.
