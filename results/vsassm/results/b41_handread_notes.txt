Failures in this run: ARGMAX 961/1000, BEAM(4,16) 484, EXACT 435, v* 716.

ARGMAX (read 4 traces in full, 15 headers). Every failure is a DEAD chain: one of the 13 mentions on the chain is
misread (typically a cluster-mate at 0.6-0.99 confidence) and the committed entity has no fact with the next
relation. With 13 readings at a 26% per-mention error the chain survives in about 0.74^13 = 2% of queries plus the
cases where both readings of an entity are misread the same way. Nothing in the traces suggests a scoring problem.

EXACT (read 5 in full, 15 headers). 13 of the first 15 failures return the true answer's cluster-mate: (a) the last
object mention is confidently misread (q1 'Dutriam Surdiahiam' -> Surdiathiam 1.00, truth 0.00; likewise q5, q11,
q18, q22): no arm can recover this from the given distributions; (b) the start is misread and the decoy chain exists
(q3: start p(true) = 0.07, decoy=True -> EXACT follows the decoy to its end 'Biktar Falgum'). q0 returns an
unrelated entity: hop-3 subject 'BruusbrfGijnpflom' is read as a different cluster at 0.98 (truth 0.00), so the true
path has no mass. These are naming-head failures (overconfident misreadings), not propagation failures.

BEAM(4,16): the same near-miss pattern as EXACT on 12 of 15 (often the identical wrong answer), plus 3 DEAD chains
(q9, q13, q19) where the truth fell outside a mention's top-4 (EXACT also misses q9, returning a cluster-mate).

v* (VSA_HOP4_D65536_kb4; read 3 in full, 15 headers): 13 of 15 failures return an UNRELATED entity (another
cluster), only 2 a cluster-mate (q11, q12). Full traces show the mechanism diagnosed on dev: where a hop's two
readings of the same entity overlap little (q6 hop 1: start p(true) = 0.05, subject reading 0.97 on a cluster-mate
-> overlap <p, ps> about 0.04; q5 hop 1: subject reading 0.16/0.09 spread), the surviving mass is a few percent of
the query's mass, the VSA's signal is proportional to that unnormalised mass while crosstalk from the 7-17 facts of
the relation is proportional to the whole query, and the clean-up picks noise. EXACT renormalises over the surviving
path and answers these queries correctly (q6, q2, q15 are not among EXACT's failures).

Summary: table failures are near misses forced by the naming distributions (irreducible for any arm given these
readings); VSA failures are mostly crosstalk picks of unrelated entities, i.e. the VSA loses the chain-breaking
evidence that a table gets for free by renormalising. The failure-type shares over all test queries are in
results/b41_report.txt.
