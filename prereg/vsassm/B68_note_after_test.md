## What the abstract's coupling sentence may and must not say

Current: "Maps from frozen language models' states to VSA vectors failed without identity labels; with labels the memory
answered almost every question, as a dictionary did (on Qwen3-8B from an exploratory middle layer)."

**Must not say:**
- that maps from frozen states failed without identity labels, unqualified: from the input embeddings of both models and
  from Phi-2's layers 2 and 4, label-free readers matched the identity-labelled head in arena A (R 0.92-1.02), and on
  Qwen3-8B layer 2 one recovered 60% of the gap;
- that label-free coupling succeeded by learning from the memory's answers: the readers that worked are clusterings of
  the frozen states that the answer loss did not improve (vq degraded with training; dkvb at L0 equals hashing the
  token vectors);
- anything about arena C at early layers (not run), or that the identity labels "did the work" at the input or early
  layers (there a hash of the written tokens did as well);
- (Results, section 2.1) "readers of input embeddings or early layers were not tested".

**May say** (with the numbers above):
- that label-free readers failed from contextual states: from Qwen3-8B's layer 4 (at most 24%, interval to 32%) and from
  the final layers of both models and Qwen3-8B's layer 27 tested before (B42, B48, B52: the seven interfaces at most 11%;
  B31's contrastive map 27% on Phi-2 in arena A);
- that where a symbol's state still identifies its written form, at the input embeddings (both models) and through layer
  4 of Phi-2, clustering the states without labels coupled them to the memory as well as the identity-labelled head did
  (hashing the written tokens, which needs no model, reaches the same: 0.975 / 0.995 in arena A), and that this capacity
  fell with depth on Qwen3-8B (100%, 60%, 24% of the gap at layers 0, 2, 4);
- so identity labels were needed to read symbols from contextual states, not to couple a frozen model to a VSA memory as
  such. A sentence of this kind fits the abstract, e.g.: "Maps from frozen language models' contextual states to VSA
  vectors failed without identity labels; from the input embeddings (and Phi-2's first four layers), clustering the
  states sufficed, as hashing the written symbols did." It must not say "early layers" without the model: on Qwen3-8B
  layers 2 and 4 recovered only 60% and 24%.
- The supplementary note "Label-free interfaces" and Fig. 2's caption ("Codes read from a frozen model's contextual
  states needed identity labels") need the layer qualifier and this table; the B42 / B52 numbers stand for the layers they
  tested.
