# What is not in this package, and why

Generated by `build_deposit.py` from `deposit_manifest.json`. Sizes are of the source files.

## Listed by experiment

- **Per-question outputs of the B18 reader on QA6-QA10 and at 512k / 1M tokens (b18_test/student_s0/*), and its development runs (b18_dev/)** (BABILONG). Where: PACE scratch: $PACE_SCRATCH/vsassm/b18_test, $PACE_SCRATCH/vsassm/b18_dev (not on the machine that built this package). Why: not available locally; QA1-QA5 at 0k-128k are in results/vsassm/b23_ref/b18_s0 (copied for B23), the frontier subset in results/vsassm/b18_api/ours_subset.json, 10M in results/vsassm/b18_10M, QA11-13 in results/vsassm/b18c_test; aggregated tables in results/vsassm/results/b18_verdict.txt.
- **LoRA adapters of the BABILong reader: b18_models/student_s0 (the reader of every BABILong number except QA11-13), b18_models/student_b18b_s0 (B18b) and b18_models/student_b18c_s0 (B18c, QA11-13); and the end-to-end fine-tunes of B23 (b23_models/Y_s0 primary, Y_s1, N_s0). All are LoRA r=16, alpha=32 on the q/k/v/o/gate/up/down projections of Qwen3-1.7B (base model Apache-2.0), 17.4 M parameters each; trained by b16_train.py (pace/b18_distill.sbatch, pace/b18_student.sbatch, pace/b18c.sbatch; preregs B18, B18b, B18c) and b23.py (pace/b23_run.sbatch; prereg B23)** (BABILONG). Where: PACE scratch ($PACE_SCRATCH/vsassm/b18_models/, $PACE_SCRATCH/vsassm/b23_models/); not on the machine that built this package. Why: not available locally; estimated 35 MB (bf16) to 70 MB (fp32) per adapter, about 210-420 MB for all six, so the three readers (B18 s0, B18c s0, B16 s0) fit the 300 MB budget if the authors retrieve them.
- **Shipped reader adapter b16_models/student_s0 (LoRA r=16, alpha=32, q/k/v/o/gate/up/down projections of Qwen3-1.7B; base model Apache-2.0; trained by b16_train.py, prereg B16)** (OPLOGS_LOCATION). Where: PACE scratch ($PACE_SCRATCH/vsassm/b16_models/student_s0) and the A800 workstation; not on the machine that built this package. Why: not available locally; estimated size 35 MB (bf16) to 70 MB (fp32), 17.4 M parameters.
- **Reader training data (model-written stories and teacher labels of B15/B16)** (OPLOGS_LOCATION). Where: b16_data on PACE. Why: regenerable with b16_stories.py and b16_label.py; not needed to check any reported number.
- **The rest of the nsengine repository (its later experiments NSR6, NSR7 and NSR10-NSR12 and other documents)** (SOLVER). Where: nsengine (private). Why: not used by the manuscript.
- **Trained naming heads and projections of the arenas (Phi-2 base, MIT)** (CONTROLLED). Where: not saved by the arena scripts as far as could be determined; if banked, on the A800 workstation. Why: not located; each head is a linear layer (2,560 x vocabulary) retrained in minutes from the cached frozen states by the arena scripts.
- **B41's per-instance run files (runs/*.npz of the store and table arms)** (COMPOSITION). Where: the A800 workstation (/path/to/work/b41, set in b41_jobs.py); not on the machine that built this package. Why: not retrieved; the verdict, the development choice, the scores and the rescoring that the paper reports are in results/vsassm/results/b41_*.

## Removed by exclusion rules

- BABILONG, vsassm: 2 files, 98.0 MB (e.g. `b18_api_subset.jsonl.gz`). b18_api_subset.jsonl.gz holds the BABILong inputs (PG-19 book text) sent to the APIs; b18_data holds the reader's training sentences (bAbI/BABILong text with teacher labels). Both are regenerated by b18_distill.py / b18_api.py from the public datasets.
- BENCHREFEREE, vsassm: 4 files, 303.9 MB (e.g. `b29_out/ruler/test_full_prompts.jsonl`). RULER prompts with their haystack essays (third-party text, 295 MB); regenerated by the RULER scripts as recorded in prereg/vsassm/B19_ruler_prereg.md and assembled by b29_ruler.py
- BENCHREFEREE, vsassm: 312 files, 762.2 MB (e.g. `b29_out/logs/calls_dev_gpt61low.jsonl`). the parts of B29 and B30b on simulated operational logs, which the manuscript assigns to another study (Supplementary Information, Pre-specified hypotheses); their code is the operational-log reader and simulator, not used by any reported number
- CLUTRR, vsassm: 13 files, 424.4 MB (e.g. `r1_data/train.csv`). r1_data/r2_data/r2b_data are the CLUTRR splits (CC BY-NC 4.0) or stories built from them; the NSR0 prediction dumps (about 320 MB) are regenerated by nsr/nsr0*.py and are summarized in nsr0_summary.json and the verdict
- EQUALBITS, vsassm: 179 files, 2186.8 MB (e.g. `b32_out/fitcache/qwen8_whiten_pq8_64.faiss`). fitted FAISS product-quantization indexes (fit caches of the index arms, 1 to 70 MB each, about 2.2 GB in all); refitted by b46_run.py / b49_run.py / b49_fam.py from the training split's codes with the seeds in the scripts
- EQUALBITS, vsassm: 4 files, 173.2 MB (e.g. `b60_out/step5/step5_M1024_V16.pkl`). pickled development and test predictions of every least-squares configuration for B60's step-5 agreement check (prediction P11; 173 MB; pickles are also unsafe to load from an untrusted archive); regenerated on CPU by `python3 b60_step5.py M V` for (M, V) in (256, 2), (256, 16), (1024, 2), (1024, 16); the chosen configurations' per-query outputs are in b60_out/ and the agreement is reported in results/b60_* and the B60 verdict
- OPLOGS_LOCATION, vsassm: 80 files, 36.6 MB (e.g. `apps/real/data/realdata/jira_apache/SPARK.json`). realdata belongs to later real-data studies not in the manuscript; out/test2 is the discarded comparison run without the asset list (README 'discarded as unfair'); page/ is a showcase web page
- OPLOGS_NSR, vsassm: 4 files, 0.2 MB (e.g. `nsr/data/ops_nsr1smoke_dry.jsonl`). smoke-test dry runs
- RULER, vsassm: 16 files, 218.8 MB (e.g. `b21_dl/b21_test.tgz`). the .tgz and the uncompressed .jsonl are duplicates of files that are included; prompts_dev.jsonl holds RULER haystack text; frontier/lme* belong to the LongMemEval experiment (B20), which the manuscript does not report
- SECONDIMPL, secondimpl: 16 files, 8.6 MB (e.g. `cleanroom2_lam/paper/ms.txt`). full copies of earlier drafts of the manuscript and Supplementary Information, the text each clean room worked from; superseded by the manuscript
- SECONDIMPL, secondimpl: 1 files, 11.3 MB (e.g. `cleanroom2_ldpc/out/tables.pkl`). the LDPC clean room's pickled code tables (11 MB), rebuilt by its code/table.py

## Removed everywhere

- Model weights and checkpoints (`*.pt`, `*.safetensors`, `*.bin`, `*.ckpt`), numeric arrays (`*.npy`, `*.npz`: the encoders' code matrices, fitted product-quantization caches and other intermediates, each regenerated by the deposited code as stated in the experiment notes of README.md; kept where an array is the only per-query record, namely B66 part (A)'s and the classical-memory clean room's `corr_*.npz` and `senscorr_*.npy`), caches (`__pycache__`, `.pytest_cache`), archives (`*.tgz`, `*.zip`; they duplicate included files, except B65's predictions archive, whose members are packaged unpacked) and credential files (`.env`, `*_key`, `*.key`, `*.pem`). No base-model weights are redistributed; the scripts download Qwen3 (Apache-2.0), Qwen2.5 (Apache-2.0 / Qwen license), Phi-2 (MIT), Granite-3.3 and Mistral-7B (Apache-2.0), Llama-3.1 (Llama 3.1 Community License) and the sentence encoders from the Hugging Face Hub.
- Symbolic links inside output directories (second paths to files packaged under their own path).
- Third-party long contexts: RULER haystacks (Paul Graham essays), BABILong book text (PG-19), CLUTRR stories (CC BY-NC 4.0). Where a per-question file carried them, the field is replaced by its length and SHA-256 (`<stripped: N characters of third-party text, sha256 ...>`), so that a regenerated input can be checked against it. Sample ids, questions, gold answers, predictions and scores are kept.
- Git histories of the source repositories. `ledger/` carries commit metadata only.
- Session transcripts and private notes of the authors and of the AI assistant.

## Left out to keep the package to what the manuscript uses (2026-11-28)

- run logs, job logs and smoke tests: 1848 files, 5.1 MB (e.g. `results/vsassm/b27_out/logs/diag_probe.txt`).
- internal review, audit and defect notes: 8 files, 0.1 MB (e.g. `code/ngcls/AUDIT_2026-10.md`).
- full commit logs: 3 files, 1.0 MB (e.g. `ledger/ngcls_commits.tsv`).
- figure style note: 1 files, 0.0 MB (e.g. `figures/STYLE.md`).

None of these files is a source of any number in the manuscript, the Supplementary Information or the figures (every figure pointer still resolves; `python3 tools/check_package.py`). The GPU-hour tallies that the Supplementary Information cites are kept (`results/vsassm/b40_out/test/logs/gpu_hours.txt`, `results/vsassm/b50_out/logs/gpu_hours.txt`). Run logs and the full commit histories are available from the authors on request.
